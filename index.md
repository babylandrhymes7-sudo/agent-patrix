# Agent Patrix

"What If?" 3D science explainers: an extreme event happens to *you*, and everything it does is shown on your body in one continuous take. Posted as YouTube Shorts on the AgentPatrix channel, Instagram Reels on [@trivenza.live](https://www.instagram.com/trivenza.live/) and Reels on the TriVenza Facebook Page.

Agent Patrix uses a small private tool, run on the channel owner's own computer, to upload its own videos to its own YouTube channel, Instagram account and Facebook Page, and to read those accounts' view and retention numbers.
